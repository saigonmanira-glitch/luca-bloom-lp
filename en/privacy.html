Privacy policy

This policy explains how Luca Bloom (“we”, “us”) handles personal information on its official website (https://luca-bloom.com/, the “Site”). It is written to meet the UK GDPR, the UK Data Protection Act 2018 and the Australian Privacy Act 1988.

2. What we collect

  • If you email us: your email address, your name (if you give it) and the content of your message.
  • When you visit the Site: technical data that your browser sends automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, referring page and the date and time of access. These are recorded on the servers of the hosting service listed in section 5.

The Site does not use cookies. There are no accounts or purchases on the Site, and we do not collect your name, address or payment details through it.

3. Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To reply to your enquiries. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you, or taking steps at your request.
  • To run the Site securely and protect it against misuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the Site available and secure.
  • To count page views and referring sites in aggregate, so we can improve the Site. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in understanding how the Site is used. This uses no cookies and does not identify you.
  • To comply with the law. Legal basis: legal obligation.

4. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and any follow-up, and no longer than 3 years. Hosting access logs are kept by the hosting provider under its own retention policy.

5. Services we use and international transfers

To understand how the Site is used as a whole (for example, how many times each page is viewed and which sites visitors come from), we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It does not use cookies or store anything on your device, and Cloudflare states that it does not identify or “fingerprint” visitors by IP address or browser details. We also use Google Analytics (Google LLC) to understand how pages are used. On the pages for the UK and Australia it runs in a mode that does not store cookies or other identifiers on your device. It receives information such as the page viewed, the referring site, clicks on the buy buttons, your browser and device type and an approximate location derived from your IP address. Google Analytics data is not used for advertising. See How Google uses information from sites that use its services. To deliver and measure the Site we use the following services. When you view a page, technical data such as your IP address is sent to them.

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States): hosting of the Site
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., United States): delivering the Site through its network and counting page views (Cloudflare Web Analytics)
  • Google Analytics (Google LLC, United States): analysing how the Site is used, without cookies

We are based in Japan, and these providers are based in the United States, so your data is processed outside the UK and Australia. Japan has a UK adequacy regulation and an EU adequacy decision; transfers to the United States rely on the providers’ own safeguards. Please see each provider’s privacy policy for how it handles data.

6. Links to Amazon

The “Buy on Amazon” buttons link to product pages on Amazon. When you buy, Amazon handles your name, address and payment details under its own privacy notice. We do not receive this information through the Site.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of, correct, delete or restrict the use of the personal information we hold about you, to stop using it, or to transfer it to you. Contact us using the details below; we will verify your identity and respond within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) in the UK or the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) in Australia.

8. Security

We take appropriate measures to protect against the loss, misuse or unauthorised disclosure of the personal information we hold.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the law changes. The updated policy applies from the time it is published on this page.
