Before you start

  • For adults only: you must be at least 18, or the age of majority in your state, whichever is greater.
  • For external use on the foreskin only. Never insert the arms into the urethra (the urinary opening).
  • Before first use, check the list “Do not use this product if any of the following apply” in the PDF (section 4).

  1. ① Before and after use

    Wash with a mild detergent

    Before first use (required) and before and after every use, wash it with a mild detergent and water, then dry it completely. To disinfect it, use isopropyl alcohol.

    Before every use, check the product, especially the arms and the release plate, for cracks or damage. Do not use a damaged product.

  2. ②

    Apply cream or oil, then insert the tips

    Apply plenty of cream or oil to the arms and the skin. With the arms closed, slowly insert the tips a short distance into the opening of the foreskin, only as far as needed to touch its inner edge. Take care not to pinch skin or hair.

  3. ③ 30 minutes at most

    Turn the knob slowly to open

    Turn the knob slowly to open the arms, and stop well before the point of pain. The arms stay at that width when you let go.

    During use, check the glans and foreskin regularly for a change in color, such as reddish-purple or darker. Stop right away if you notice one.

    Time limits (for safety) 30 minutes at most per session, and 1 hour at most in total in any 24 hours. Never use it while sleeping.
    Do not open too far Never open it to a point that causes significant pain (bottom right of the picture).

  4. ④

    Close the arms, then remove

    Turn the knob in the direction of the CLOSE arrow engraved on the side of the body to close the arms, then remove the device slowly. After use, wash it as in step ①, including any cream left on the lead screw (the threaded rod).

  5. Storage

    Keep away from sunlight, heat and humidity

    Store it at room temperature in a clean place. Keep it out of reach of children.

  6. Do not

    Do not boil it or use hot water

    Boiling or hot water can deform the product. Disinfect it with isopropyl alcohol instead.

  7. EMERGENCY

    If it will not come off

    Do not pull it.

    If the knob turns: turn it in the direction of the CLOSE arrow. The arms close and the pressure eases; in most cases you can then remove the device as usual.

    If the knob will not turn, or the device still will not come off: do not force it. Slide the release plate (the flat, keyhole-shaped part at the right end of the body) in the direction of the RELEASE arrow shown in the picture, so that the large round opening of the keyhole lines up with the shaft. The shaft passes through and the arms come free.

    If it still will not come off, do not use any more force. Seek medical attention promptly. In an emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

Stop using it right away

  • If you notice pain, congestion, swelling, numbness, a change in color or anything unexpected, stop right away and seek medical care.
  • If you feel discomfort, first turn the knob in the direction of the CLOSE arrow to ease the pressure. Do not pull the device.
  • If the foreskin has been drawn back behind the glans and will not return to its normal position, do not leave it: seek emergency medical care immediately.
  • If the device breaks during use, stop, remove it and recover all the pieces. If a piece remains in the application area, or you cannot confirm that you have recovered every piece, do not try to remove it yourself: seek medical attention promptly. Do not use a product that has broken.
  • If you notice redness, irritation or any other skin reaction, stop using it.
