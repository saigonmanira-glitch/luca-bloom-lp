4 reasons

Why choose Luca Bloom

REASON 01

Reverse-taper arms that stay in place

Each arm is narrow at the base and gets wider towards the tip (a reverse taper). The neck is 5 mm wide when closed. Because the tip is wider, the tool is less likely to slip during use, and its flat faces spread contact instead of pressing on a single point.

REASON 02

Flat-faced arms that are kinder to skin

Each arm is 6 mm wide. The outer face that touches the skin is flat, with its corners rounded to R2 (a 2 mm radius). Instead of pressing along a single line like a round rod, it makes contact across a surface, so the same force is spread over a wider area.

Figure: cross-section of the arm at its thickest point (to scale)

REASON 03

Stepless adjustment. Stop exactly where it feels right.

Turn the handle and the arms open smoothly. There are no set steps, so you can stop at precisely the width that suits you. A screw mechanism holds that position (self-locking), so the arms won’t snap shut when you let go.

Safety

Important safety information

Please read before first use.

Before use

  • For adults aged 18 and over. Keep out of reach of children.
  • Wash with mild detergent and water before first use and before and after every use, then dry thoroughly. You can disinfect it with alcohol.
  • Do not boil it or use hot water, as this can deform the tool.
  • Check the arms for cracks before each use. Do not use a damaged tool.
  • Do not use on broken, inflamed or infected skin, or if you are receiving treatment for the area. Ask a doctor first.

During use

  • Apply plenty of cream or oil, and take care not to trap skin or hair.
  • No more than 30 minutes at a time and 1 hour in total in any 24 hours. Never use while asleep.
  • Check the colour of the skin regularly.

Stop immediately and seek medical advice if

  • you notice pain, congestion, swelling, numbness or any change in skin colour (such as turning purple or dark);
  • retracted skin does not return to its normal position (seek urgent medical attention);
  • the tool breaks during use. Remove it, collect every piece and do not use it again.

Removing the tool

  • Turn the handle in the direction of the “CLOSE” arrow on the side of the body to close the arms, then remove it slowly.
  • If the handle won’t turn, do not pull. Slide the release plate (the flat, keyhole-shaped part at the end of the body) in the RELEASE direction so that the large round hole lines up with the shaft. The shaft comes out and the arm is released.
  • If you still cannot remove it, do not apply more force. Seek medical help right away.
